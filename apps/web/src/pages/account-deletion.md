## In the app

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Type **DELETE** to confirm.

Altus offers to export your transactions as a CSV file first.

## Without the app

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] **from the email address you sign in with** and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm, then delete it within [NUMBER] days [LEGAL CHECK].

## What is deleted

Straight away: your profile, accounts, transactions, imports, categories, budgets, goals, streaks and notification settings. You're signed out on every device.

## What stays

- **Shared groups**: if other people joined a group with you, the group passes to another member and keeps your name and what you paid, so everyone else's balances stay right. A group nobody else joined is deleted.
- **Backups**: encrypted backups expire within 30 days.
